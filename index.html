SEMINARIO DE INVESTIGACIÓN I

Maestría en Geomática

Universidad Nacional de Colombia - Sede Bogotá

Research Seminar I provides a space for academic encounter and discussion. It facilitates students' engagement with the scientific, social, and environmental context, enabling them to identify a research problem and formulate pertinent research questions.

Research seminars for master's degrees are academic activities designed to provide students with the necessary tools for the preparation of the Thesis Project and the execution of the Thesis, in the case of research master's degrees; and for the preparation of the Final Work Proposal and the Final Work, in the case of professional master's degrees ( Acuerdo 04 de 2023- Consejo Superior Universitario)
